隱私權政策

Feiyu（飛予）個人 AI 助理 · 最後更新：2026-10-05

適用對象

Feiyu 是 SmallYuan 個人自用的 AI 助理，只會存取擁有者本人授權的 Google 帳號，不提供給其他人使用。

存取哪些資料

經擁有者授權後，Feiyu 可透過 Google API 存取擁有者帳號中的 Gmail、Google 日曆、Google 雲端硬碟、文件、試算表與聯絡人，用途僅限於：

  • 依擁有者的要求讀取、整理、搜尋與寄送郵件
  • 查詢與管理行程
  • 讀取與編輯擁有者的文件

資料如何處理

  • 授權憑證只儲存在擁有者自己的電腦上。
  • 處理請求時，相關內容可能會傳送給擁有者選用的 AI 模型服務以產生回覆。
  • 不會出售、出租或與任何第三方分享資料，也不會用於廣告。

Feiyu 使用 Google API 取得的資料，遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其中的 Limited Use 規定。

撤銷存取

擁有者可隨時在 Google 帳戶的第三方存取權頁面撤銷授權，並刪除本機儲存的憑證。

Privacy Policy

Feiyu personal AI assistant · Last updated: 2026-10-05

Feiyu is a personal AI assistant used solely by its owner, SmallYuan. It only accesses the Google account its owner has authorized and is not offered to anyone else.

With the owner's consent, Feiyu accesses the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts through Google APIs, only to read, organize, search and send the owner's email, manage the owner's schedule, and work with the owner's documents at the owner's request.

Credentials are stored only on the owner's own computer. Content relevant to a request may be sent to the AI model provider chosen by the owner to generate a response. Data is never sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Feiyu's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account third-party access.
